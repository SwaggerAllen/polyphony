Moderation

An internal tool for people making judgement calls under time pressure, which means density is fine and ambiguity isn't. Everything here is already built except the part that lets anyone report something — the queue has never had an input.

00 · Four things this screen has to get right
Child safety is its own lane. Not a filter on a general queue — a separate list that is always first, always visible, and doesn't get buried under forty spam reports. Different urgency, different handling.
Reading a report means bypassing publication scope. A moderator has to see everything to judge, including perspectives the author never shared. That's a real privilege and the screen should say so out loud rather than granting it silently.
Content and people are different objects. Taking down a snapshot and suspending an account have different consequences and different reversals. Never one button.
A take-down takes everything, and spreads. Public copy and the author's own, plus every fork descended from it — which can't be deleted blind, because a fork may have diverged past anything objectionable. So a take-down opens a review lane rather than firing a cascade.
