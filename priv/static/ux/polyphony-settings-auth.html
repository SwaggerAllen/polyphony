Polyphony · step 13 · settings & auth

Account, money, and getting in

Not glamorous, but two things here are load-bearing: spending caps you can actually change, and a signup that asks for an age attestation and then uses it.

00 · Two things worth fixing here
Caps are referenced but not editable. Error copy already says "you can raise it in Settings" and there is nothing in Settings to raise. Two limits, both real: a rolling daily one on you, and a lifetime one per campaign.
18+ is eligibility, not a content setting. Under-18s can't use Polyphony at all — supporting them would mean parental controls and in-house filtering, which is a large piece of work deferred a long way out. So an unchecked box ends signup rather than limiting it.
Spend is split by who it's for. Your daily limit protects you from a runaway loop. A campaign's lifetime limit protects you from one story quietly eating the month. They fail differently and belong in different places — the campaign one lives in campaign settings.
Turning someone away costs us nothing to store. A blocked signup should keep no personal data at all — a local flag on the device is enough for a best-effort block, and holding anything else about someone we've just refused would be the wrong trade.
Magic link only. No passwords to store, reset, or leak. The cost is a longer first-time flow, so the check-your-email state has to be good.
